DATA COLUMNS EXPLANATION (Zomato / Restaurant Dataset)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Based on the provided sample data, here is a short description of each column:

1. address             : The physical location/address of the restaurant.
2. name                : The official name of the restaurant.
3. online_order        : Categorical (Yes/No) indicating whether online ordering is available.
4. book_table          : Categorical (Yes/No) indicating whether table reservation is available.
5. rating              : Numerical rating of the restaurant (typically out of 5).
6. votes               : Total number of customer reviews or ratings submitted for the restaurant.
7. location            : The specific area or neighborhood where the restaurant is situated.
8. cuisines            : Types of food/cuisines served (can contain multiple items separated by commas).
9. cost_per_person     : Average cost for one person dining at the restaurant.
10. type_of_restaurant : The style or theme of the restaurant (e.g., Buffet, Cafes).
